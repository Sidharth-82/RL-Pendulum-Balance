## Training

### Learning curve

Sampled training return against the deterministic evaluation score. Evaluation uses the action mean, not a sample.

![Learning curve](media/training-report/return.svg)

> The gap between the two is the whole story of the collapse: a greedy policy can sit at ceiling while the stochastic policy it is actually training on dies early.

### Episode length

Steps survived. Read alongside return -- a rising return with short episodes means the agent found a way to end early, not a way to balance.

![Episode length](media/training-report/length.svg)

### Policy entropy

Exploration left in the Gaussian head. Decay toward zero is what removes the policy's ability to absorb a large update.

![Policy entropy](media/training-report/entropy.svg)

### Approximate KL per update

Trust region. The trainer early-stops the epoch loop when this crosses 0.02.

![Approximate KL per update](media/training-report/kl.svg)

### Clip fraction

Share of sampled actions whose probability ratio hit the PPO clip. Sustained high values mean the step size is too large for the data.

![Clip fraction](media/training-report/clip.svg)

### Epochs actually run

Ten unless the KL early-stop fired. Every dip below ten is an update the trainer judged too aggressive to finish.

![Epochs actually run](media/training-report/epochs.svg)

### Policy loss

Negated surrogate, so descending is the policy improving. Near zero is expected -- it is a clipped ratio objective, not a fit error.

![Policy loss](media/training-report/ploss.svg)

### Value loss

Critic fit against the GAE returns. Falls as the value function learns the episode is worth roughly the ceiling.

![Value loss](media/training-report/vloss.svg)

## Behaviour

### Tilt from upright

Deterministic evaluation rollouts. The link starts hanging at 180 degrees; the task is to reach and hold 0.

![Tilt from upright](media/training-report/tilt.svg)

### Cart position

How much rail each policy spends. Leaving the rail ends the episode.

![Cart position](media/training-report/cart.svg)

### Commanded acceleration

Raw network output, logged before the environment clamps it. Rapid sign flips during the hold are a limit cycle at the control rate -- the behaviour that chews through a real belt.

![Commanded acceleration](media/training-report/action.svg)

> The trainer logs `action_mean` straight off the network, so excursions past +/-1 are real: an untrained policy asks for several times the available acceleration and the env saturates it. Everything the plant actually saw is inside the clamp lines.

### Phase portrait, best policy

Iteration 820. Angle against angular rate, the full 8 s rollout: one sweep from hanging into the origin, then nothing.

![Phase portrait, best policy](media/training-report/phase.svg)

## Derived per evaluation

### Swing-up time

First moment the tilt enters the 15 degree cone and never leaves it again. Blank where the policy never got there and stayed.

![Swing-up time](media/training-report/swing.svg)

### Tilt at the end of the episode

Steady-state error after 8 s. This is the number that separates 'gets upright' from 'holds still'.

![Tilt at the end of the episode](media/training-report/finaltilt.svg)

### Command reversals during the hold

Share of consecutive control steps that flip the command sign, over the last 2 s. High values are the limit cycle; it is invisible in the return.

![Command reversals during the hold](media/training-report/reversal.svg)

### Mean command magnitude during the hold

Average |action| over the last 2 s. Driven to zero by the effort penalty, which is a hundred times smaller than the alignment term.

![Mean command magnitude during the hold](media/training-report/effort.svg)
